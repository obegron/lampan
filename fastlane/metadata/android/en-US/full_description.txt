Lampan captures playback audio from Android apps and streams it directly to
AirPlay receivers on your local network.

Features include:

* AirPlay 1 support for speakers such as IKEA Symfonisk and Sonos
* Native encrypted AirPlay 2 for compatible receivers such as Sony Bravia TVs
* Automatic receiver discovery and remembered device capabilities
* Multi-receiver streaming with manual synchronization adjustments
* Background streaming and volume control
* Secure local storage for successfully verified receiver access

Lampan has no accounts, advertisements, subscriptions, analytics, or cloud
relay. Audio and receiver control traffic remain on your local network.

Android playback-capture restrictions still apply. Apps can prevent their audio
from being captured, and Lampan does not bypass DRM protection.
